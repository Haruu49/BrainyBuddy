How it works

Four steps, and the picture gets clearer with every exam and study session you add.

  1. Set up your profile

    Choose your education level, add your subjects and pick the goals that matter to you.

  2. Practise or import

    Take a timed practice exam in the simulator, or upload results from exams you've already sat.

  3. Track your study

    Start a timer when you study. Log breaks and rate your focus when you finish.

  4. Review and act

    Check the patterns, follow a recommendation, and watch its progress against a clear target.

Evidence first, never guesswork

Brainy Buddy describes what happened in your data, not why you did it. When there isn't enough data, it says so.

  • Every insight lists the numbers it is based on.
  • Confidence depends on how much data there is.
  • No study order or habit is assumed to be right or wrong.
  • Targets are measurable, so you can see progress.

Demo and privacy notice

This is a frontend demo of Brainy Buddy. Sign-in is simulated: accounts, exams, study sessions and imported records are saved only in this browser's local storage.

Nothing is sent to a server. Recommendations come from transparent rules, not a connected AI model. You can export or delete your demo data at any time from Settings.
